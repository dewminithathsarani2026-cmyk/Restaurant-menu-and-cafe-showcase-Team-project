     🌄 Sun Rise

                        ABOUT US

A small cafe,built slowly,on purpose.

Sun rise started as a single roasting kettle and a borrowed kitchen. Eight years on, it's a corner where regulars know the staff by name, and the bread is still shaped by hand every morning.




OUR STORY

Why we started

It began with a question one of our founders kept asking: why does the coffee shop near the train station taste like every other coffee shop?

So we did the slower thing instead. We found a roaster who'd talk for an hour about altitude and soil. We learned to laminate croissant dough by hand, badly, then less badly, then well. We kept the tables mismatched on purpose, because a cafe should feel collected, not decorated.

What you taste today, the toasted-hazelnut filter blend, the sourdough that takes three days, the soup that changes with whatever's at the market, comes from that same stubbornness. We'd rather make less and mean it.
